Season cumulative HDD (Sep-Apr)

Observed: ERA5 reanalysis (archive lags ~5 days), bridged to yesterday with the Open-Meteo analysis. Dashed tail = latest ECMWF ENS mean. Normal = mean cumulative path of the 1991-2020 seasons cached so far (ERA5 history backfills two years per run).
Method: daily mean 2 m temperature per city from Open-Meteo (ECMWF IFS 0.25° ENS, NCEP GEFS 0.25°, ECMWF AIFS ENS; every member), weighted by approximate temperature-sensitive residential + commercial gas demand by country (UK, Germany, Italy, France lead), split across each country's main load centres. HDD = max(0, 65°F − T). Weights are a demand proxy, not an official index. Source: scripts/europe/europe_weather.py.
